### **1. Primary Pain Problem Solved**
Civil service candidates face a fragmented, high-stakes system where application windows are extremely short (often 5–10 business days) and published across 200+ distinct agency portals in unsearchable PDF formats. Missing a single relevant announcement means waiting **12 to 24 months** for the next examination cycle.

| **Not Knowing Eligibility** | Disqualification at document review or interview stage due to complex OCSC major accreditation rules. | **HIGH** |

## PHASE 3 — 299 THB VALUE TEST

### **1. Why would they pay 299 THB/month?**
- **Insignificant Cost vs. Lifelong Return:** Starting civil service salary (ข้าราชการ) ranges from 15,000 to 18,000 THB/month with medical coverage for life and pension benefits. Paying 299 THB/month (roughly the cost of 2 cups of premium coffee) during a 3–6 month job search is a trivial investment for a lifelong career asset.
- **Time Capital Reallocation:** Saves 40+ hours per month of manual website hunting, allowing candidates to spend that time preparing for the competitive ภาค ข. / ภาค ค. examinations.

### **2. What would make them cancel?**
- **Getting Appointed / Passing Exam:** Once a user passes the exam and accepts a civil service post, they no longer need the product. (Healthy churn).
- **False Negative Alerts:** If a candidate discovers a job on Facebook that VERIIGO failed to index, trust drops immediately.

### **3. What single feature creates the strongest retention?**
**Smart Deadline Protection & Personal Alert Engine.**  
Candidates live in constant anxiety of "What if a job opened yesterday and closes on Friday?" The automated, high-precision push alert gives peace of mind.
