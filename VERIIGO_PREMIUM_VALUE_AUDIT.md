# VERIIGO Premium Value Audit & Monetization Strategy

**Product Architecture:** VERIIGO Career Intelligence Engine  
**Target User Segment:** Thai Government Job Seekers (ข้าราชการพลเรือนสามัญ, พนักงานราชการ, รัฐวิสาหกิจ)  
**Subscription Price:** 299 THB / Month (Or 2,490 THB / Year)  
**Audit Status:** Final Strategic Review  

---

## 1. Value Proposition Audit (Question 1: Would a job seeker pay monthly for this?)

### **YES. A job seeker will pay 299 THB/month because:**
1. **High-Stakes Career Outcome:** Becoming a government officer (ข้าราชการ) provides lifelong career security, healthcare benefits, and pension. Spending 299 THB/month during a 3-6 month job hunt is negligible compared to a lifelong career return.
2. **Shift from "Listing Aggregator" to "Personalized Intelligence":** Users do not pay to browse public job announcements. They pay because VERIIGO filters, cross-references, and ranks positions **specifically for their exact degree, major, OCSC exam status, and location preference**, ensuring they apply before missing deadlines.
3. **Prevention of Costly Disqualifications:** Applying for a position without meeting OCSC accreditation or exact major requirements results in travel costs, examination fee loss, and wasted preparation months. VERIIGO's **Explainable Match Engine** flags qualification gaps in advance.

## 2. Core Pain Points Solved (Question 2: What painful problem does it solve?)

| Pain Point | Traditional Method (Government Websites / Facebook Groups) | VERIIGO Premium Career Intelligence Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fragmented Announcements** | Scattered across 200+ ministry websites, PDF announcements, and social media posts. | **Centralized Registry & Coverage Engine:** Automated scraping, document extraction, and structured indexing. |
| **Complex Eligibility Rules** | 100-page PDF announcement specifications with ambiguous OCSC major matching. | **AI Job Match Score Engine:** Transparent, component-by-component match calculation (0-100%). |
| **Missed Application Deadlines** | Users realize an announcement closed 2 days ago after seeing it on social media. | **Smart Alert System:** Proactive notifications for >85% matched positions with deadline countdowns. |
| **Unclear Application Odds** | No idea if a position with 1 vacancy is worth taking leave from work to sit for the exam. | **Application Success Intelligence:** Opportunity level ratings (HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW) based on vacancies and competition risk. |

---

## 3. Consequences of Not Using VERIIGO (Question 3: What happens if user does not use VERIIGO?)

1. **Missed High-Probability Opportunities:** Job seekers miss announcements that match their exact niche degree because the announcement was posted on an obscure provincial office bulletin board.
2. **Disqualification at Document Verification Stage:** Applicants waste weeks studying for the ภาค ข. exam, only to be rejected at the interview stage because their university major was not OCSC-accredited for that position code.
3. **High Stress & Time Drain:** Candidates spend 10-15 hours per week manually refreshing dozens of agency websites instead of focusing on exam prep.
